# Third-party notices

This helper bundles [Opus](https://opus-codec.org/) from the version pinned in
`scripts/package-macos.js`. Opus is distributed under its BSD-style license.
That license and its notices govern libopus code distributed with a published
helper.

The release build uses the pinned libopus version recorded by
`scripts/package-macos.js`. Packaging copies this notice and
`OPUS-LICENSE.txt` beside the helper and bundled dynamic library. Keep the
exact libopus version, architecture, linkage mode, and corresponding notices
with every redistributed app bundle.
